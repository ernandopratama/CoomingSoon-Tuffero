## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory precision and timeless decadence of luxury French haute pâtisserie. Balancing dramatic darkness with opulent warmth, it mirrors the atmosphere of an intimate Parisian atelier: rich cocoa tones, glowing champagne leaf accents, and delicate ivory porcelain surfaces.

### Aesthetic Movement & Philosophy

The aesthetic blends high-editorial luxury minimalism with warm, tactile elegance. It avoids cold corporate sterility and hyper-futuristic gloss, choosing instead an artisanal, curated mood:

- **Atmospheric Chiaroscuro:** Deep cacao surfaces serve as the canvas, establishing high visual contrast against warm ivory elements and liquid champagne gold accents.
- **Editorial Grandeur:** Display typography relies on high-contrast Bodoni serifs to convey bespoke craftsmanship, balanced by clean, geometric modern grotesque forms for functional interfaces.
- **Restrained Ornamentation:** Subtle, hairline borders, soft micro-radii, and ambient golden glow evoke hand-crafted packaging, gold-leaf gilding, and tailored presentation boxes.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in rich culinary indulgence and artisanal gold-leaf craftsmanship.

### Core Swatches

- **Primary / Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`):** The signature accent. Used for key callouts, active states, delicate iconography, and primary conversion triggers. It embodies refined luxury and celebratory indulgence.
- **Neutral / Dark Chocolate (`#2B1B14`):** The primary canvas and dark foundation. A deep, warm, cacao-infused black that provides an intimate, enveloping atmosphere without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary / Roasted Brown (`#6D4C3D`):** A warm, mid-tone brown used for secondary structural tiers, muted containers, hover states on dark surfaces, and subtle visual dividers.
- **Surface Light / Warm Ivory (`#F6F0E5`):** High-contrast readability surface and prominent card canvas. It evokes whipped cream, warm porcelain, and textured luxury pastry paper.
- **Border / Subtle Trim (`#E3D5C0`):** Soft, warm outline tint used at variable opacities (15% to 40%) for delicate divider lines, input borders, and framing accents.
- **Pure Highlight (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved strictly for razor-sharp micro-highlights, pristine focal points, and maximum clarity badge text on gold surfaces.

### Tonal Application

- **Base Background:** Deep Dark Chocolate (`#2B1B14`).
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Roasted Brown (`#6D4C3D` at varying opacities) or contrasting Warm Ivory (`#F6F0E5`) for distinct physical cards and presentation blocks.
- **Text & Foreground:** Warm Ivory (`#F6F0E5`) for text on dark backgrounds; Dark Chocolate (`#2B1B14`) for text placed atop Warm Ivory containers.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between editorial romance and modernist clarity.

### Hierarchy & Pairings

- **Headlines & Display (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers dramatic vertical contrast, sharp serifs, and classical luxury. Used for heroic proclamations, collection titles, and sensory tasting notes. Headings use tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain editorial cohesion.
- **Interface & Prose (`Manrope`):** Delivers geometric precision, open counters, and effortless legibility in dense UI layers, specifications, and nutritional or ingredient notes.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in `Manrope` uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury packaging stamps and couture labeling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy champions generous, unhurried space, evoking high-end editorial magazines and bespoke boutiques where products are given room to breathe.

### Grid & Composition

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid grid bound to a max-width of `1440px`. Column gutters set to `2rem` (`space-lg`) with outer screen margins of `4rem` (`margin-desktop`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`gutter`) with outer margins of `2rem` (`margin`).
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single or dual-column stacked reflow with `1rem` (`gutter-sm`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`) margins.

### Rhythmic Cadence

Components observe strict rhythmic multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`). Micro-details (tags, small buttons) leverage `4px` (`space-xs`) precision padding. Section transitions use generous `space-2xl` offsets to enforce an atmosphere of calm, intentional exclusivity.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is tactile, warm, and ambient—mimicking soft spotlighting over dark polished marble and dark cacao woodwork.

### Depth Techniques

- **Ambient Golden Diffusion:** Instead of harsh black dropshadows, elevated floating elements use warm, tinted atmospheric shadows:
  - Low elevation: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 11, 8, 0.6), 0 0 1px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.15)`
  - High elevation: `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(12, 7, 5, 0.8), 0 0 20px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.08)`
- **Hairline Framing:** Surfaces are enclosed with delicate 1px border strokes using `#E3D5C0` set to `12%–25%` opacity, replicating gold filigree edging on luxury ballotin boxes.
- **Warm Ivory Card Layering:** Selected focal cards intentionally reverse the dark palette by lifting forward into Warm Ivory (`#F6F0E5`). These surfaces feature warm ambient roasted drop shadows (`rgba(43, 27, 20, 0.12)`) and crisp dark chocolate typography.

## Shapes

The design language selects Level 1 (Soft) roundedness.

### Geometric Rationale

- **Architectural Tailoring:** Base elements feature a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving the sharp, tailored silhouette of handcrafted chocolate tablets and structural gift packaging.
- **Card Containers:** Primary containers, light cards, and modals scale up gently to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), softening the silhouette while maintaining an architectural presence.
- **Chips & Badges:** Rounded to `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`) or fully pill-shaped when expressing tasting notes or limited-edition tags, creating a soft tactile contrast against geometric framing.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) solid background with Dark Chocolate (`#2B1B14`) typography in `label-lg` uppercase. Hover state deepens to a burnished gold with a delicate outer bloom (`0 0 16px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Dark Chocolate canvas with a 1px border in `#C6A15B` (60% opacity) and Warm Ivory text. Hover fills to Roasted Brown (`#6D4C3D` at 30% opacity) with 100% border luminance.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with Warm Ivory text and an animated champagne hairline underline.

### Cards

- **Dark Reserve Card:** Background in Roasted Brown (`#6D4C3D` at 20% opacity) with a 1px `#E3D5C0` border (15% opacity). Features Bodoni headline in Warm Ivory, champagne price indicators, and ambient cocoa depth.
- **Ivory Patisserie Card:** High-contrast presentation card with solid Warm Ivory (`#F6F0E5`) fill, Dark Chocolate (`#2B1B14`) typography, and a hairline border in `#E3D5C0` (50% opacity). Used for signature degustation highlights.

### Input Fields

- Dark Chocolate base with a 1px border in `#E3D5C0` (25% opacity).
- Warm Ivory typography with placeholder text softened to `#F6F0E5` at 40% opacity.
- Focus state illuminates the border with solid Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) accompanied by a subtle 2px blur glow (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.2)`).

### Chips & Tasting Badges

- Compact padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-md` horizontal).
- Dark cocoa backing with 1px gold hairline trim. Text set in `label-sm` tracking with Champagne Gold or Warm Ivory coloring.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)

- Base boxes in `#2B1B14` with a 1.5px `#E3D5C0` (30% opacity) perimeter.
- Checked state fills with solid Champagne Gold (`#C6A15B`) featuring a Dark Chocolate checkmark or center pip.

### Lists & Menus

- Items separated by subtle hairline rules (`#E3D5C0` at 10% opacity).
- Menu items transition on hover with a warm Roasted Brown (`#6D4C3D` at 25% opacity) highlight and gold indicator dash.
